Updates
1. Adjusted z-curve scoring — elite seasons can now score above 100 (up to 120) for historically dominant performances
2. Improved role classification — elite scorers (top 20% pts/GP) are now correctly identified as Snipers or Playmakers
3. Generational offensive performers (200+ projected points) now provide a pull-up effect that can offset other roster weaknesses
4. Reworked mobile layout — draft roster and player tiles now fit cleanly on small screens, and results show record + full roster on a single shareable screen
5. Playstyle roles (Sniper, Playmaker, Two-Way, etc.) now visible on player cards during the draft and in the roster panel
6. Re-run Simulation button added to results screen — roll the dice up to 3 times on the same roster before drafting again
7. Bad-player drag penalty added to the sim engine — weak players (bottom 25% of the league) now cost escalating win probability: -2% for one, -6% for two, -12% for three, -20% for four or more
